## Patterns to copy
1. Real car cabin (headrests, seatbelts, door frames visible)
2. Hands clipping headrest straps (install proof)
3. Dog naturally jumping/sitting on hammock
4. Mesh window / side flaps close-ups
5. Waterproof wipe or muddy paws proof
6. Short bold text, UGC pacing (~15s)
