## Winning ad formula (what competitors repeat)

1. **0–3s hook** — Muddy dog jumps into clean car / ruined leather seats (problem flash)
2. **Problem** — Hair, scratches, dirt, dog leaping into front seat
3. **Reveal** — Cover unrolls / snaps onto headrests in under a minute
4. **Feature demos (fast cuts)**
   - Waterproof wipe / water beading
   - Mesh window (dog sees owner → less anxiety)
   - Zippered side flaps (door protection + easy entry)
   - Storage pocket (treats/leash)
   - Seatbelt tethers / non-slip
5. **Lifestyle close** — Happy dog on road trip
6. **CTA** — “Shop now” / “Protect your seats today”

## Claims competitors hammer

- Waterproof / 600D Oxford / scratch-proof
- Mesh window for airflow + anxiety reduction
- Zippered side flaps / door protection
- Easy 1-minute install (headrest straps)
- Universal fit (cars, trucks, SUVs)
- Storage pocket
- Multi-mode (hammock ↔ bench ↔ cargo) — Coverado “5 styles”
- Full floor coverage / hard bottom (Kurgo floor guard, Amazon hard-bottom)

## Visual styles that convert

- UGC: phone footage of muddy dog + install in driveway
- Split-screen before/after dirty seat
- Close-ups of quilting, mesh + orange trim, zippers, buckles
- Dog looking through mesh toward driver
- Open-door side shot showing zipped hammock barrier

## Our creative angle (differentiation)

Your SKU already matches the winning feature set. Emphasize what photos show best:

1. **Orange-accent mesh + pocket** — clearer visual brand cue than plain black Amazon clones  
2. **Diamond quilting** — PetSafe-style “premium comfort” feel vs flat Oxford  
3. **Hammock + door flaps + seatbelt clips** — full protection story in one install  
4. **Muddy-paw wipe proof** — strongest TikTok/Meta hook in this category
